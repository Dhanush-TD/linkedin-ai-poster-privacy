Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI LinkedIn Auto Poster ("the Application") handles information when you use the Application to create and publish content to LinkedIn.

1. Information We Access

The Application may access information provided by LinkedIn when you authorize the Application, including information necessary to authenticate your LinkedIn account and publish content on your behalf.

The Application may also process content generated for LinkedIn posts, including text, images, topics, and related publishing information.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through LinkedIn is used only to:

  • Authenticate the LinkedIn account connected to the Application.
  • Create and publish LinkedIn posts on the user's behalf.
  • Maintain the status of generated and published posts.
  • Prevent duplicate content and manage the automation workflow.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services that are required to operate the Application, such as AI model providers, hosting providers, databases, and LinkedIn APIs.

4. LinkedIn Data

The Application uses LinkedIn APIs according to the permissions granted by the user. LinkedIn data is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable LinkedIn developer terms and policies.

5. Data Storage

Authentication credentials and application data may be stored securely using appropriate technical safeguards.

Access tokens and other sensitive credentials are not intentionally exposed publicly in source code or public repositories.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Application, maintain publishing history, and provide the requested automation functionality.

7. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with the Application by contacting the application owner.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect application credentials and user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

9. Third-Party Services

The Application may interact with third-party services including LinkedIn and AI or hosting providers. Their respective privacy policies and terms may also apply to information processed by those services.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Application's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of application data, please contact the application owner.
